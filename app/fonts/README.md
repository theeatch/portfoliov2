# Fonts

All three typefaces are free, libre fonts from the [Velvetyne Type Foundry](https://velvetyne.fr),
released under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (the licence texts sit next to the files).
They are served from here with `next/font/local` (see `app/layout.tsx`); nothing is loaded from a CDN.

| File | Typeface | Designer | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Daubenton-Regular.woff` | Daubenton | Olivier Dolbeau | Headings, nameplates, engraved labels. Capitals only: a revival of the letters carved on the façade of the Great Evolution Gallery, Natural History Museum, Paris. |
| `BluuNext-BoldItalic.woff2` | Bluu Next Bold Italic | Jean-Baptiste Morizot | The italic accent phrase in headings. |
| `HappyTimes-*.woff2` | Happy Times (Regular, Italic, Bold) | Lucas Le Bihan | Body text and tempo markings. |

Sources: Daubenton and Bluu Next from github.com/velvetyne, Happy Times from gitlab.com/velvetyne/Happy-Times-at-the-IKOB.
